The Edit

Three passes, one document.

The same process runs behind every case study below — it's what turns a duty list into a document a hiring manager reads twice.

01 — INTAKE

The draft comes in

Your work history as it exists today — messy, incomplete, or just too modest. Nothing gets thrown away; everything gets mined for proof.

02 — REDLINE

Duties become evidence

Every vague line is interrogated: what changed because you were there? Numbers, scope, and outcomes get pulled out and made visible.

03 — APPROVED

A document that argues for you

Structured, keyword-matched, and built to be scanned in six seconds by a human or a filter — and to hold up once someone actually reads it.
